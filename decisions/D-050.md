# D-050 The connections window

Topics: [[window]]

(the owner, 2026-11-10) The connections window follows the reference program's layout: the list on the left with Import… and Export… under it, the fields of the selected connection on the right with New, Save and Connect, and a splitter between them that is kept with the window's size and place. Connect, Duplicate and Delete are in the right-click menu of a connection. Duplicate saves a copy with its password, named "… (copy)", ready to be renamed. Why: the fields can be made narrow, and a copy never overwrites its original, as Save as could.

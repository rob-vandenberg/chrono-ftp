# D-047 When the queue is done

Topics: [[window]], [[transfers]]

(the owner, through the Proceed on block FC) A choice in the queue panel says what happens once nothing is waiting, running or paused any more and the last item ended by itself: stay, disconnect the connections the queue used, close Chrono FTP, suspend or shut down the computer, the last three after a 30 s countdown that can be cancelled. It applies once and goes back to Stay; a choice the computer does not allow is greyed, and edited files not yet on the server keep Chrono FTP open and the computer on. Why: a long transfer can end the session by itself without anything being lost.

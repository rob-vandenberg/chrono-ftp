# D-071 Toolbars

Topics: [[window]]

(through the Proceed on block FQ, 2026-11-13) A right-click on any toolbar (the top bar, the folder and file buttons of each side, the queue buttons) or on the empty part of the top row opens one menu, through open_menu ([[D-052]]): show or hide each toolbar, small, medium or large icons (medium is the size each toolbar had before, 24 pixels on the sides), "Text under this button" for the button right-clicked, and "Restore the standard layout". The choices are kept in the settings at once; a hidden toolbar's keys keep working, as they belong to the lists they act on. Why: the reference program's toolbars can be arranged, and the person must never lose a key or the way back.

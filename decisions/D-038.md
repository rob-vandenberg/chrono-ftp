# D-038 Synchronize

Topics: [[actions]]

(the owner, changed through the Proceed on block FF, 2026-11-13) Synchronize compares the two folders shown, with everything below them, and shows every difference as a checklist of planned actions: a tick, what it would do, and the size and date of both sides. The person chooses the direction (to the server, to this computer or both ways), the mode (Synchronize, Mirror or Timestamps only, [[D-091]]) and what two files are compared by ([[D-092]]); nothing happens until Apply, which runs through the queue ([[D-095]]). Synchronize may now also delete: a delete row is always shown, "Delete on the other side" decides only whether it starts ticked, and Apply names how many items will be deleted and asks first ([[D-094]]). Links are left out. Why: comparing was postponed until the owner asked for the whole program; a mirror is worth nothing without deleting, and the step that can't be undone is always seen before it happens (G-2). The owner's review (0.0.44): the button that does the work reads Start, and the same button reads Abort while it runs ([[D-097]]).

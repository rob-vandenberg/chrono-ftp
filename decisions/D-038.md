# D-038 Synchronize

Topics: [[actions]]

(the owner) Synchronize compares the two folders shown, with everything below them, by size and date, so it works on every connection. The person sees every difference and ticks what is copied; only what is one-sided or clearly newer starts ticked. Nothing is ever deleted, and links are left out. Why: comparing was postponed until the owner asked for the whole program; deleting is the step that can't be undone.

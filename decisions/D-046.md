# D-046 Queue control per item

Topics: [[transfers]], [[window]]

(the owner, through the Proceed on block FC) Every item of the queue can be paused and resumed (what arrived is kept, so Resume continues from there), moved up or down, run now, retried where it can continue, stopped, deleted, and given a speed limit of its own that holds at once; Stop All, Pause All, Resume All and Clear Finished act on the whole queue. Done items leave the list after a while, stopped and failed ones stay until the person acts; an error goes on with the next item or pauses the queue, as Settings say; closing waits for every transfer thread within a bounded time. Why: the person decides what runs, when and how fast, and nothing that arrived is lost by a pause.

# D-034 Each side has its own buttons

Topics: [[window]]

(the owner) Each side has one row of buttons, icons only with the text on hover, for walking (Back, Forward, Parent, Root, Home, Refresh, Filter) and for its actions, a path line that can be typed in, and a count line under the list. The tabs and the general buttons share one row above both sides. The columns are Name, Size, Changed, Rights, Owner, Group and, on the right, Link target; the status bar shows the protocol, a lock and the connected time. Why: the owner's picture of the goal; a button that belongs to a side leaves no doubt what it acts on.

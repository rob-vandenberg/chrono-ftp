# D-034 Each side has its own buttons

Topics: [[window]]

(the owner) Each side has its own row for walking (Back, Forward, Parent, Root, Home, Refresh, Filter), its own row of actions, a path line that can be typed in, and a count line under the list. The columns are Name, Size, Changed, Rights, Owner, Group and, on the right, Link target; the status bar shows the protocol, a lock and the connected time. Why: the owner's picture of the goal; a button that belongs to a side leaves no doubt what it acts on.

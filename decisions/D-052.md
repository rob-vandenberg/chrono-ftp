# D-052 Menus hold the mouse, not the keyboard

Topics: [[window]]

(the owner, 2026-11-10) Every right-click menu opens through one place, which lets go of the keyboard once the menu is open and keeps the mouse. Why: on X11 an open menu otherwise holds the whole keyboard, so the desktop's own shortcuts, such as a screenshot of the open menu, did nothing; software adapts to the person, not the other way round. Menus also have a border, room around them and roomy items, as in Chrono Mail.

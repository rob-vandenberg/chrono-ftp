# D-078 Clipboard copies and the path in the title bar

Topics: [[window]]

(through the Proceed on block FG, 2026-11-13) The right-click menu of a list has Copy to Clipboard: the folder path, the selected names (also Ctrl+Shift+C) or their full paths, one per line, each in double quotes where it holds a space. The title bar shows "Chrono FTP 0.0.40", then " - " and the folder of the right side: its last folder (the standard), the whole path, or nothing (Settings, General). Why: the reference program's panel export and path in the caption.

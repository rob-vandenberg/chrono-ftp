# D-067 Clean up stored data

Topics: [[window]]

(through the Proceed on block FP, 2026-11-12) "Clean Up Stored Data…" on the General page of Settings lists what Chrono FTP keeps, each kind with its size and place and a box: saved connections with their passwords, trusted servers, bookmarks and location profiles, window places and settings, notes of unfinished transfers with leftover edit copies, session logs, and the terminal's colour schemes. Nothing is ticked at first; Remove asks first and removes exactly what is ticked. Why: a person who stops using the program, or hands on the computer, can leave nothing behind.

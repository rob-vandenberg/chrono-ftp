# D-093 The scope of a scan and its masks

Topics: [[actions]]

(through the Proceed on block FF, 2026-11-13) A scan can be limited to the names that are already on both sides, to the two folders without their subfolders, and to the files the person had selected. On top of that the mask engine of block FD decides what is looked at, with include and exclude patterns read relative to the two folders, so one mask means the same on both sides, and "Skip empty folders" leaves out a folder with nothing in it ([[D-081]]). The progress bar of a scan uses the file count of the last scan of the same two folders, kept per folder pair. Why: a scan of a whole tree is slow, and the person usually knows what part of it matters. Review round 1: a scan can be stopped from the same button that starts it, as Compare Contents is; a stopped scan leaves the rows as they were, leaves nothing running, and says so calmly.

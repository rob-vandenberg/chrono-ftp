# D-041 The main window in parts

Topics: [[window]]

(the owner, through the Proceed on block FA) The main window is one class, `MainWindow` in `ui/mainwindow.py`, built from part classes, one module per group of actions: layout, tabs, remote folders, edit, queue, actions on this computer, actions on the server, transfers. What they share (widgets, state, numbers, and the methods one part calls in another) is declared in `ui/windowbase.py`; one tab with its edited files is in `ui/session.py`. `mainwindow.py` itself only builds the window and its toolbar and closes it. Why: every block after FA adds to the window, and each file must stay under 2,000 lines (OR-8); the parts keep the methods on the window, so nothing the person sees changes.

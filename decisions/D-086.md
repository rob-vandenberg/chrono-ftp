# D-086 The filter window

Topics: [[window]]

(through the Proceed on block FD, 2026-11-13) The filter of a side is a small window with the mask field and its history, Filter and Clear. Only the files that pass the mask show; folders always stay in view. The active filter shows next to the path of its side: a click on it opens the window, the button beside it clears it; the "N filtered" count opens it too. Each tab keeps the filter of both sides. Why: the reference program's filter with its mark next to the path, and every tab is its own place ([[D-008]]).

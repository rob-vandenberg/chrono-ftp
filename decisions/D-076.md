# D-076 The status line of a side

Topics: [[window]]

(through the Proceed on block FG, 2026-11-13) Under each list: the size of what is selected of the size of all files, then the count of what is selected of all entries ("1.2 MB of 3.4 MB, 2 of 10"), "at least" before a size where the server gave no size for a file, or, while typing, what was typed. Beside it, "N hidden" where files whose names start with a dot are hidden, or "N hidden, shown" where they show; a click switches it on both sides and keeps it, as Ctrl+Alt+H does. "N filtered" shows where the filter leaves entries out; a click opens the filter. Why: the reference program's status bar of a panel and its clickable hidden and filtered panels.

# D-048 Several transfers at once

Topics: [[transfers]], [[window]]

(the owner, through the Proceed on block FC) Up to "Transfers at once" items run side by side (default 2, 1 to 9, in the queue panel and Settings), each on a thread and, over FTP, a login of its own; over SSH they share the tab's login. An item never runs while another works on the same place, a server that takes no more logins makes an item wait calmly, only one overwrite question is shown at a time, and the status bar's speed limit is shared by the running items. Why: many small files and several servers go much faster, without two transfers ever writing the same place or a server refusing work halfway.

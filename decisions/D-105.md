# D-105 The look: dark, light or the desktop's

Topics: [[window]]

(through the Proceed on the light look, 2026-11-14) Chrono FTP has two looks, dark and light, and a **Look** choice on the General page of the settings offers The same as my desktop, Dark and Light; a fresh installation follows the desktop, and so does an updated one, because a settings file from before this version holds no look, and a desktop that tells the program nothing means dark. Following the desktop means following it while the program runs, so turning the desktop light turns Chrono FTP light at once; a person who chose Dark or Light keeps it whatever the desktop does. Changing the look never waits for a restart: the palette, the menus and the icons are drawn again, the tab bar works out its greys while it paints, and what keeps a picture or a palette of its own is told again: the right side, the lock in the status bar and the colour blocks of the connection list. Both looks live in `src/chrono_ftp/core/colours.py` and nowhere else, as two complete sets of the same names, and reading the desktop brings in one word, light or dark, never a colour, so nothing comes from the platform (OR-9). An icon takes the colour of the text at the moment it is drawn, which is what makes a change of look leave no icon behind in the old colour. The colours a person chooses stay exactly as they chose them on both looks, both a connection's colour and the colours of a file rule, with black or white text picked by the brightness of the background ([[D-007]]); only the colours the program *offers first* for a new file rule follow the look. Why: a program that is dark on a light desktop looks broken, and the person who prefers the other one should not have to live with ours.

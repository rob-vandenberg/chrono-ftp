# D-042 A closed window takes no more answers

Topics: [[window]]

(the owner, through the Proceed on block FA) Once the person agreed to close the window, nothing more happens in it: the bridge that hands the answers of the connection threads to the window is closed, and an answer that comes later, or is still on its way, goes nowhere. Why: a thread that finished after the window was gone handed its answer to a deleted object.

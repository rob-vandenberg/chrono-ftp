# D-092 What two files are compared by

Topics: [[actions]]

(through the Proceed on block FF, 2026-11-13) Synchronize compares by date (with the size), by size, by checksum or by name only, and a choice says whether upper and lower case are different names. The checksum way uses the checksum function of block FE, so it works wherever the connection computes checksums, and is greyed with its reason elsewhere ([[D-088]], OR-20). Dates are compared at the step the listing reports them in, found from every date the scan saw on the other side (whole minutes, even seconds, or seconds), in place of the fixed two-second margin of before. Why: a server that reports minutes made every file look different, and the person decides how sure a comparison must be.

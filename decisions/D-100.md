# D-100 Workspaces

Topics: [[window]]

(through the Proceed on block FI, 2026-11-13) A workspace is every open tab under a name, each with the folders both its sides were in; they open again in one go, in the order they were saved, which wins over the order the tabs were dragged into ([[D-098]]). A right-click on an empty part of the tab row saves, opens, renames and deletes workspaces and chooses the one that opens by itself when Chrono FTP starts; with no tab open, saving is greyed with its reason (OR-20). Saving under a name that is taken asks before it replaces, renaming to a name that is taken changes nothing and says so, and deleting asks first and leaves the tabs open (G-2). A workspace opens again exactly the tabs it saved, two tabs on one connection included: a tab that is already open covers one of them and the rest are opened beside it ([[D-098]]), so Connect still never opens a second tab of its own ([[D-051]]). A connection that is no longer in the connection list is named in the status bar, with what did open. Where a workspace opens at start, the connections window stays shut, as the window is already full of tabs. The workspaces live in `workspaces.json` next to the other settings; a damaged file gives no workspaces and a damaged entry is left out (OR-15). Why: the reference program's workspaces, and the owner opens the same handful of connections every day.

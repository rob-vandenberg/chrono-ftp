# D-039 The queue

Topics: [[window]]

(the owner) The queue sits under a draggable separator, its height kept. It shows while a transfer runs, hides a few seconds after the last one, and stays when pinned with the pin on its title bar; the pin is kept too. After a failed transfer it stays open until the person closes it with the Queue button, so a failure is never hidden. Why: every pixel of height belongs to the file lists.

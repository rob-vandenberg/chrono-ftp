# D-039 The queue

Topics: [[window]]

(the owner; extended through the Proceed on block FC) The queue sits under a draggable separator, its height kept. It shows while a transfer runs, hides a few seconds after the last one, and stays when pinned with the pin on its title bar; the pin is kept too. After a failed transfer it stays open until the person closes it with the Queue button, so a failure is never hidden; it also stays while items are paused or a step is chosen for when the queue is done. Its title bar holds the queue's buttons, "Start items by themselves", "Transfers at once" and "When done" ([[D-046]], [[D-047]], [[D-048]]). Why: every pixel of height belongs to the file lists.

# D-084 Transfer presets

Topics: [[transfers]]

(through the Proceed on block FD, 2026-11-13) The Presets page of Settings keeps named sets of transfer settings (New, Rename, Duplicate, Delete), each with rules on the host, the user, the remote folder and the local folder, each a mask of the whole value. The transfer window, and a transfer without it, starts with the first preset whose rules all fit, else the settings of the Transfers page, named "Standard"; the window names it and lets the person choose another, whose settings show at once. A preset without rules is chosen only by hand. In a folder rule `~` is the home folder of its side, and a rule ending in `/*` fits that folder itself and everything below it. Why: the reference program's transfer presets with their rules.

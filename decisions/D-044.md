# D-044 Part files, their notes, resume and tidying

Topics: [[transfers]]

(the owner, through the Proceeds on blocks FB and FC) A transfer writes to a part file of our own next to the target, and notes on this computer which source it holds and how many of its bytes are confirmed (for a file in parts also the ranges confirmed after them, [[D-049]]); a part a pause kept is noted to be continued whatever the resume setting, until the pause ends otherwise than by Resume ([[D-046]]); a part is continued only from that very source, unchanged, and is ours only with its note, so a real file that merely carries our suffix is never touched. A part is kept only after a stop, a lost connection or a problem of this computer's disk, and tidying removes it once nothing can continue it; every name of our own stays within 255 bytes. Why: a broken transfer can go on where it stopped without ever mixing two files or touching someone else's.

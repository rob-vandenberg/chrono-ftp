# D-043 Transfer options and the transfer window

Topics: [[transfers]], [[window]]

(the owner, through the Proceeds on blocks FB and FC) Every upload, download and drop first shows the transfer window: the target folder, the options in one line, Transfer settings for this one transfer, and a tick to delete the source once it arrived; Settings keeps the defaults and can switch the window off. The options are resume (Smart, Always, Never), ask before replacing, only new and updated, keep dates, rights for uploads, count first, and splitting large files over several connections ([[D-049]]); what a connection can't do is greyed out and says why, and a move is never kept as a default. Why: the person sees and chooses what happens before anything is sent, and nothing fails halfway on a server that can't do it (OR-7).

# D-085 Selecting by mask

Topics: [[window]]

(through the Proceed on block FD, 2026-11-13) Num+ selects by mask and Num− unselects by mask on the list with the keyboard focus (OR-21), also as Select by Mask… and Unselect by Mask… in the Select submenu; a small window has the mask field with its history and "Folders too". Files are chosen by the file patterns, folders by the patterns ending in `/`, and with "Folders too" also by the others; the rest of the selection stays. A mask with only a part after the `|` (`| *.bak`) chooses every file but what it leaves out, the same meaning the filter gives it, and folders only with "Folders too". With "Folders too", every pattern after the `|` leaves folders out as well. The plus and minus of the main keyboard still find names as before; those of the number pad never do, and where their action is greyed they do nothing and the status bar says why. Why: the reference program's Select and Unselect by mask on the keypad keys.
